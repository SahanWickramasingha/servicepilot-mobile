Delete your ServicePilot account and associated data

Email sahanwikrama29@gmail.com with subject ServicePilot Account Deletion Request. You can request deletion without reinstalling or signing into the app. Send from your registered email when possible and say whether you use a customer or technician account. Do not send passwords, tokens, private keys or unnecessary identity documents.

Sending your request

The email button opens your mail composer; it does not submit a request. Review and send the email yourself. If no mail app is available, copy the address or use your webmail. The owner will need to verify account ownership before acting and should explain the deletion scope and any retention exceptions.

Scope and current limitations

The request covers Firebase Auth, profile/public projections, locations, associated Firestore records and stored media, including review photos, request/job photos and signature evidence. Shared bookings, other participants' records, safety/audit records and provider copies require individual review rather than blind deletion. No verified fixed retention period or guaranteed processing time has been published. The current administrator delete action disables account access and does not erase Auth, Firestore or media. Full verified erasure and provider-copy handling still require operator/backend work; do not treat a disabled account as completed deletion.
