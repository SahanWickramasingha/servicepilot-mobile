Accounts and profiles

We process name, email, phone, address, account role, verification/account status, and registration Terms acceptance and Privacy acknowledgement versions/time. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in credentials and verification; passwords are not copied into public profiles. Technician profiles also contain specialty, experience, qualifications, certifications, declared service districts/areas, optional profile photo and availability. Account and job data support the service you request; security and moderation data protect participants and service integrity. Applicable statutory processing grounds and any legal retention duties depend on where the service is offered; contact the owner with questions. Optional location and media features use separate user actions and device permissions rather than relying on registration acknowledgement as consent.

Public and private information

Signed-in eligible customers can browse approved technicians' public directory projections: name, specialty, experience, qualifications/certifications, service areas, availability, optional photo and rating/job totals. Reviews can show the reviewer's name, rating, comment, date and published review photos to eligible signed-in readers. Do not put private address or contact details in public profile text or reviews. Private user documents are restricted to their owner, authorized super administrators, and dispatchers reviewing technician profiles. Street/home addresses and private precise GPS are not copied into the public map projection. Authorized job participants and super administrators can access job records/evidence according to role and job permissions; dispatchers do not assign jobs.

Optional location

Technician sharing is off until enabled. While sharing in the foreground, device GPS is stored privately and rounded to a grid for customer map browsing. Eligible accepted/assigned/in-progress job customers can access that technician's fresh precise shared location; other browsing customers see only an approximate area. OFF and account/logout cleanup revoke sharing; a network failure can delay confirmation and is reported in the app. Stale locations are labelled. Background tracking is not enabled. Declared service areas are separate from GPS. A customer's optional foreground fix is held in app memory for approximate straight-line distance, not saved remotely for browsing. You can refuse permission and still browse.

Bookings, uploads and signatures

Bookings include selected participants, service description, address/contact, schedule/status, cancellation and work-timer information. Customers may select request photos; technicians may take before/after photos and enter diagnosis, work, parts and recommendations. A technician may capture a customer's drawn signature and approval for completed work. The signature is job evidence; the app does not perform biometric identification from it. Request/job media are restricted to authorized participants under the storage provider's rules; job evidence is also available to authorized super administrators. Published review photos have broader signed-in visibility. Notifications include personal job events and permitted system messages; read receipts track their read state. Reports and private block lists support moderation and your display/booking preferences.

Providers and security

Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Firebase Storage support accounts, records and review/legacy media. Configured Cloudflare Workers support trusted booking operations and authenticated private photo access; R2 stores scoped request/job photos. Google Maps renders the Android map. Its Android SDK can automatically process device/SDK metadata, crash metrics, IP address and an SDK-specific pseudonymous identifier for service improvement; map interaction events may also be processed depending on SDK configuration. Expo and React Native provide the app runtime and device APIs. These providers may process network/device and operational information as part of their services; their own terms also apply. The app uses authenticated access, role/job checks, server-generated acceptance timestamps, separate public/private location documents, bounded queries and private media references. Review images are resized before upload. There is no claim of absolute security, a particular hosting region, or that provider logs/backups are erased instantly. Do not send credentials in support messages.

Retention, correction and deletion

There is no verified fixed automatic retention period for account/job history, reviews, signatures, moderation records or provider copies. Correct profile details in Personal Information, turn location sharing off, manage device permissions, and use Support & Contact to request access, correction or deletion. Request account deletion in the app or email sahanwikrama29@gmail.com without the app. Send from your registered email when possible; never send a password, token, private key or unnecessary ID documents. Opening an email composer does not submit a request: you must send the email. The owner must verify identity, review associated Firestore/media/Auth records and explain any justified retention of shared job evidence, safety records or legal obligations. No guaranteed completion time is currently published. The existing administrator 'delete' action restricts access but is not data erasure; full erasure requires additional operator/backend handling. We cannot presently promise automatic deletion of all provider backups or logs.

Your choices and concerns

Optional location sharing can be stopped in the app; photo/camera and location permissions can be changed in device settings. These controls do not remove previously shared records. Ask the owner about withdrawing optional consent and deleting retained material. You may raise privacy concerns with the owner and, where applicable, the relevant data-protection authority. A Terms acceptance or Privacy acknowledgement is not a claim of full legal compliance or certification.
