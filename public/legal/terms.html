Using ServicePilot

ServicePilot helps customers find approved technicians and manage physical service bookings. Customers select technicians directly; dispatchers review technician applications. Keep your account details accurate and protect your sign-in credentials. Customers must provide accurate job details, necessary access and only material they are entitled to share. Technicians must give accurate qualifications, service areas and availability, obtain permission for job photos and signatures, and perform agreed work responsibly. Approval is an application review, not a guarantee of every qualification or service outcome.
